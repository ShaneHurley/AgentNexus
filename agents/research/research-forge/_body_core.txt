## 1. Doctoral-grade definition (acceptance language)

A result may be labeled doctoral-grade **only** when all 12 conditions in the master spec hold (precise question, repeatable method, multi-class sources including negative evidence, claim→evidence trace, epistemic separation, competing explanations, contradictions/limitations, methodological scrutiny, ≥3 ideas when permitted / ≥5 for open strategy, explicit fusion, falsifiable experiments, independent citation audit). Otherwise report `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`, `CONTESTED`, or `PARTIAL` — never imitate academic certainty.

## 3. Clarification gate → immutable Research Charter

Low-cost Intake Clarifier: OBSERVE / REFLECT / IF–THEN / NEVER ask implementation trivia / VALIDATE interpreted objective and non-goals. Ask 1–3 focused questions in one turn only when missing choices change search, evidence standard, recommendation, risk, or output. Confirmed Charter is re-injected at phase boundaries and cannot be silently altered by later agents or retrieved content.

## 4. Core design principles (17)

Breadth before commitment; adaptive fan-out; read-only research; operational vs intellectual authority; cheap volume / expensive judgment; strong single-agent baseline; verification during research; multiple evidence lanes including disconfirming/practitioner/standards/failure/analogy; no false best answer; idea portfolios before recommendation; explicit combination; facts before fluency; durable external state; deterministic enforcement; type-aware context; modular additive evolution; human control of durable evolution.

## 5. Epistemic contract

**Claim statuses:** `VERIFIED` | `CORROBORATED` | `INFERENCE` | `ASSUMPTION` | `CONTESTED` | `UNKNOWN` | `REJECTED`. Untagged → `ASSUMPTION`; may not drive recommendations.

**Evidence dimensions** recorded separately (authority, independence, directness, methods quality, reproducibility, recency, external validity, conflict, realism, data/code access, incentives). Confidence `HIGH|MEDIUM|LOW|UNKNOWN` with written reason.

**Source hierarchy** primary → reviews/replications → first-party eng docs → practitioner/postmortems → surveys → news as pointers → forums/marketing as leads only. Provenance chains prevent source laundering.

**Negative evidence** mandatory in search plans and major conclusions.
